Ancient India · c. 2500 BCE

Great Bath of Mohenjo-Daro

MATURE HARAPPAN · c. 2500 BCEMOHENJO-DARO · SINDHCIVIC ENGINEERING

The Great Bath is a sunken pool built with finely fitted baked bricks and sealed with bitumen.

Steps descend from both ends, while surrounding rooms suggest that bathing was more than a practical act: it may have held social or ritual significance.

This interactive model shows the bath together with its surrounding archaeological setting.

A carefully sealed pool that reveals how ancient cities made water, space and community part of one civic idea.

Key discoveries

  • Built in the Mature Harappan period
  • Measures roughly 12 by 7 metres
  • Located inside a large citadel complex
  • Water was likely drawn from nearby wells
