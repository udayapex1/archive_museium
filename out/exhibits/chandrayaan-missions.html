Modern India · 2023

Chandrayaan Missions

CHANDRAYAAN-1 · 2008CHANDRAYAAN-2 · 2019CHANDRAYAAN-3 · 2023

Chandrayaan-1, launched on 22 October 2008 aboard a PSLV rocket, orbited the Moon and helped confirm water molecules on the lunar surface.

Chandrayaan-3, launched on 14 July 2023 aboard the LVM3 rocket, achieved history: its Vikram lander touched down in the Moon's south polar highlands on 23 August 2023 at 6:04 pm IST, deploying the Pragyan rover.

The achievement made India the fourth country to soft-land on the Moon and the first to do so near the lunar south pole.

“Shiv Shakti Point” — the historic landing site where India made planetary history near the Moon’s southern pole.

Key discoveries

  • Chandrayaan-1 launched 22 October 2008; confirmed water molecules on the Moon
  • Chandrayaan-3 launched 14 July 2023 on the LVM3 rocket from Sriharikota
  • Vikram lander soft-landed near the lunar south pole on 23 August 2023, at 6:04 pm IST
  • India became the first nation to land in the lunar south polar region
  • 23 August is officially commemorated in India as National Space Day
