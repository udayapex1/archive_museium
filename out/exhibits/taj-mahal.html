Mughal Era · 1632

Taj Mahal

FOUNDATION · 1632AGRA · YAMUNA RIVERMARBLE INLAY

Commissioned by Shah Jahan for Mumtaz Mahal, the Taj Mahal was built in Agra during the seventeenth century.

Its white marble changes character with the light, while its garden, calligraphy and pietra dura inlay unite architecture with poetry and craft.

A monument to memory, where architecture, garden and light are composed as one continuous poem.

Key discoveries

  • Construction began in 1632
  • Located on the Yamuna riverbank in Agra
  • Uses marble inlay called pietra dura
  • A UNESCO World Heritage Site
