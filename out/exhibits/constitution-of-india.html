Modern India · 1950

Parliament House & Constitution of India

CONSTITUENT ASSEMBLY · 1946–1949PARLIAMENT HOUSE · NEW DELHISOVEREIGN DEMOCRACY

The Constituent Assembly, chaired by Dr.

Rajendra Prasad, began drafting India's constitution in December 1946 in the Central Hall of Parliament House.

The Drafting Committee, headed by Dr.

B.R.

Ambedkar, shaped the sovereign text establishing India as a democratic republic.

This interactive 3D model presents the monumental circular Parliament House in New Delhi where India's constitutional journey was born.

We, the people of India — a solemn resolve ensuring justice, liberty, equality, and fraternity for all citizens.

Key discoveries

  • Constituent Assembly deliberations ran from December 1946 to November 1949
  • Adopted on 26 November 1949 (celebrated as Constitution Day / Samvidhan Divas)
  • Came into force on 26 January 1950, marked as Republic Day
  • Dr. B.R. Ambedkar chaired the Drafting Committee
  • The original handwritten copies were calligraphed by Prem Behari Narain Raizada and illustrated by artists from Shantiniketan
  • The longest written constitution of any sovereign country
